Restaurant concept · Preview coming soon
Concept project

Neighborhood Restaurant

The problem: most restaurant sites bury the menu three clicks deep and look broken on phones — exactly where hungry customers are browsing.

Design direction: a menu-forward site where hours, location, and the full menu are one tap away. Online reservations up top, click-to-call on every screen, photos that actually make the food look good.

  • ✓ One-tap menu, hours & directions
  • ✓ Space for a reservation integration
  • ✓ Performance-conscious design
Contractor concept · Preview coming soon
Concept project

Home Contractor

The problem: contractors live on referrals because their websites don't generate leads — no service pages, no proof of work, no easy way to request a quote.

Design direction: a lead machine. Dedicated page per service (kitchens, baths, additions), a project gallery that sells the craftsmanship, and quote-request forms placed where interest peaks.

  • ✓ Service pages built to rank on Google
  • ✓ Before/after project gallery
  • ✓ Quote forms that feed straight to email
Fitness concept · Preview coming soon
Concept project

Local Gym

The problem: gym sites read like brochures when they should read like invitations — class times hidden in PDFs, no clear path from "interested" to "signed up."

Design direction: a site with one job: trial signups. Class schedule front and center, trainer bios with real personality, and a free-trial flow that's three fields and done.

  • ✓ Class schedule presentation
  • ✓ Clear trial inquiry form
  • ✓ Trainer bios that build trust
