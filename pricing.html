FAQ

Pricing questions, answered straight

How do payments work?

Payment milestones, any setup fee, and accepted payment methods will be confirmed in your proposal before work begins.

Do I own my website?

For one-time projects, ownership and handoff are defined in your agreement after final payment. Monthly plans include separate hosting, licensing and cancellation terms explained before signup.

What counts as a "small edit" on the monthly plan?

Common examples include text edits, hours and image swaps. New pages, redesigns and major functionality are quoted separately; we’ll define what’s included up front.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on page count, content readiness, integrations and feedback. I’ll provide a proposed schedule with your quote.

What if I already have a website?

I’ll review your current site and suggest improvements, a targeted redesign or a new build depending on its condition and your goals.

Why hand-coded instead of WordPress or Wix?

Lean HTML, CSS and JavaScript can reduce unnecessary dependencies and make maintenance more predictable. Every website still needs secure hosting, updates and ongoing testing.
