02 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Not everything fits neatly into a pricing table. Here's what clients usually want to know.

How do payments work?

For one-time projects, the standard arrangement is a deposit before work begins and the balance at launch. I’ll confirm the schedule and payment options in the project agreement.

Do I own my website?

With a one-time project, the finished website is yours after final payment. Ownership and handover details for a monthly plan depend on the agreement.

What counts as a small edit?

Examples include text changes, photo swaps, and business-hour updates. New features or major redesign work would be scoped separately.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope, feedback, and content readiness. We’ll agree on a schedule before starting.

What if I already have a website?

Updating or replacing an existing site can fit either package. I'll review your current website and confirm what can be carried over before quoting the scope.

Why choose a hand-coded website over a website builder or AI-generated site?

Website builders and AI tools can produce sites that feel generic, cluttered, or difficult to navigate. Hand-coded websites offer greater control over design, speed, and functionality. Every element is built with purpose, creating a clean, fast, and user-friendly experience that keeps the focus on your business.
