01 / INTRODUCTION

Hey, I'm Hector.

I’m the developer behind HB Web Design. I build custom websites for small businesses that want a clearer, more professional presence online.

My path has taken me from the Bay Area to New Orleans and now North Carolina. Living and working in different communities has given me an appreciation for independent businesses and the people running them.

I started HB Web Design because I enjoy the combination of design, problem-solving, and development. I like building things from the ground up and making them simple for people to use.

When you work with HB, you work directly with me — from our first conversation through design, development, and launch.
