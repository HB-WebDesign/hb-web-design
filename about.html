About

The person behind the pixels.

[Photo of Hector]

Hey, I'm Hector.

I'm a front-end developer, and HB Web Design is my shop — HB are my initials, and the skull is just what happens when you let a Stephen King reader design a logo.

I've lived and worked in the Bay Area, New Orleans, and Durham. Living in different communities showed me how much local businesses depend on a clear online presence—and how hard it can be to know what you actually need from a website.

I started HB Web Design to offer a direct, collaborative approach: thoughtful design, lean code, and honest conversations about scope and cost. You work with me throughout the project.

If that sounds like someone you want building your website, let's talk.

How I work

A few things I believe

Speed is a feature

Half your visitors are on phones, many on mediocre connections. Speed matters, so I keep assets and dependencies intentional and test on real screen sizes.

Honest scoping

I'll tell you what you need and what you don't. I’ll recommend a scope that fits your goals rather than adding features you don’t need. If I'm not the right fit, I'll say that too.

You own everything

We’ll agree on ownership, hosting, ongoing support, and how files are handed over before the project starts.

Let's see if we're a fit.

One conversation, zero pressure — I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.
